The first public version of Loadwise, a free and offline construction materials converter.

- Converter for 100 site materials: type one figure and see tonnes, cubic metres, kilograms, coverage, bulk bags, wagon loads, cost and carbon at once.
- Loose and compacted densities for every material, with the density used always on show.
- Tap any result to see the working, step by step.
- Seven calculators: aggregate and sub-base, concrete volume, tarmac and asphalt, topsoil, trench backfill, excavation and spoil, brick and block.
- Jobs and take-off, sent as a PDF or a spreadsheet file with your own company name and logo.
- Your own materials and densities, wagon planner, wastage allowance, optional imperial units.
- Light, dark and sunlight themes, a tablet layout, and a backup file you keep.
- No internet permission, no account, no adverts.

Needs Android 8 or later. Install it over an older version to keep your jobs and settings.
