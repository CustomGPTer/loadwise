Estimates only

Loadwise works from typical densities, payloads and allowances. Real materials vary with moisture, grading, source and compaction, and wagons vary with their body and axle limits. Treat every result as an estimate, and confirm quantities against your supplier's data, the drawings and the specification before you order, price or build.

Loadwise is not a design tool and does not replace an engineer, a quantity surveyor or the project specification. Where a calculator refers to a standard, the standard and the specification govern.
